**Project:** Campus Facilities Maintenance System

| Stakeholder | Role | Interests / Expectations | Influence on the System | How the System Affects Them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Students | Primary users who report campus maintenance problems | Easy reporting, quick responses, and request tracking | Their feedback influences usability and system requirements | Makes reporting and tracking maintenance issues easier |
| Faculty and Staff | Report maintenance issues in classrooms, offices, and laboratories | Reliable reporting, status updates, and timely maintenance | Influence reporting and notification requirements | Reduces the effort needed to report and follow up on issues |
| Facilities and Maintenance Staff | Receive, manage, and resolve maintenance requests | Clear descriptions, locations, priorities, and organized requests | Strong influence on workflow and request-management requirements | Helps organize, prioritize, and update maintenance tasks |
| Facilities Management | Supervises maintenance activities and service performance | Efficient task management and visibility of unresolved issues | Influences priorities, workflows, and management requirements | Provides better oversight and helps identify recurring problems |
| AUS Administration | Oversees campus operations and resources | Efficient campus services and effective use of resources | Influences policies, resources, and implementation decisions | Provides information for operational and resource-planning decisions |
| IT Department / System Administrator | Maintains security, access, and technical operation | Security, reliability, maintainability, and access control | Strong influence on technical and security requirements | Provides centralized control but creates system administration responsibilities |
| Development Team | Designs, develops, tests, and maintains the system | Clear requirements, manageable scope, and successful implementation | Directly influences system design and implementation | Provides practical software engineering experience and responsibility for delivering the system |

## Summary

The Campus Facilities Maintenance System involves several stakeholder groups with different needs and levels of influence. Students, faculty, and staff are the primary users who report maintenance issues, while facilities staff and management are responsible for processing and resolving requests. AUS administration and the IT department influence operational, security, and technical requirements. Understanding these stakeholders helps ensure that the system meets user needs and supports efficient campus maintenance operations.
